Privacy Policy

1. Data controller

Vendetta Legacy is an online game. Data you provide when registering or playing (username, email, encrypted password, in-game activity) is used only to operate the service.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: username, email and password.
  • Game data: progress, messages, alliances and actions in the universe.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and session cookies to keep your login secure.
  • If you subscribe to VIP: PayPal processes payment; we store subscription ID and renewal dates.

3. How we use it

To create your account, let you play, protect the server, enforce rules, provide support and, if you opt in, manage VIP subscription.

4. Retention

We keep data while your account is active or as required by law or game security.

5. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion by contacting game administration, except data we must keep by law or for shared-universe integrity.

6. Cookies

We use technical session cookies for secure login. We do not sell your data to third parties.
